on the historical bets, not a model retraining harness.

TR19 is certified.

DEEPER OBSERVATIONS (POTENTIAL FOLLOW-UPS, NOT SHIPPING)
--------------------------------------------------------
1. The data suggests an even tighter threshold could improve P&L further:
   * 0.20: +$32.98 (+$3.48 vs 0.22)
   * 0.18: +$41.42 (+$11.92 vs 0.22)
   But these reduce kept-bet count from 20 to 17/13 -- variance becomes a
   bigger concern at smaller samples. Wilson 95% lower bound on the
   0.18-0.22 cohort win rate (4W/3L = 57%) extends below 30%, so we cannot
   rule out that this band is genuinely break-even noise.

2. Recommended pacing: hold TR19 at 0.22 for 2-3 weeks of fresh live data,
   then re-run this backtest. If 0.18-0.22 cohort grows and remains
   negative, drop the threshold to 0.20 in a TR20.

3. The 0.15-0.18 cohort (n=13, 92% WR, +$41.42, ROI +18.4%) is the
   strongest cohort by far. Tightening below 0.15 would block real winners
   (the data shows zero kept bets at edge<=0.15), so 0.15-0.18 is the
   floor of the profitable zone.

4. p_score_event_proxy was identified in the 2026-04-28 -> 2026-05-03
   window analysis as the single strongest winner-vs-loser separator
   (wins mean 0.065, losses mean 0.000). Promoting that from a shadow
   diagnostic to a soft tie-breaker on edge>0.18 / outs>=2 signals is
   the natural next candidate for evidence-driven enforcement.
