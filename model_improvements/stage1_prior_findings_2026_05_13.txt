Stage-1 Historical Prior Findings - 2026-05-13

Scope
- Question: what historical run-environment prior should Stage-1 use for MLB totals FV?
- Motivation: uniform 10-season history added support but appeared too high-scoring for the current MLB environment.
- Data used: local regular-season MLB game feeds, calibration-opportunity rows through 2026-05-09, and cache-swapped FV ablation.

Important conclusion
- This is not a simple 5y vs 10y problem.
- The best practical prior is a relevance/support tradeoff:
  - Rolling 4y (2022-2025) best matched the current low-to-mid scoring environment on deduped all-family and no-score calibration.
  - Rolling 5y (2021-2025) remains the conservative production fallback because it has more support and preserved better score-event rank ordering.
  - Uniform 10y is too high-scoring for current conditions unless future data overturns this.
  - Weighted 10y improves over uniform 10y but did not beat 4y/5y on the current labeled sample.

Scoring-environment evidence
- 2016-2025 mean RPG: 9.064
- 2021-2025 mean RPG: 8.908
- 2025 RPG: 8.895
- 2026 YTD through 2026-05-11: 8.460
- Blended 2026 projection: 8.804
- Post-2023 slope: about -0.168 RPG/year
- 2019 and 2023 are high-scoring anchors; equal weighting lets them pull Stage-1 upward.

Family-specific read
- no_score_drift:
  - rolling4 was best on deduped Brier/logloss.
  - This supports the state-value/no-score pivot because no-score drift cares more about confirmed current run environment than immediate post-score ranking.
- score_event_transition:
  - rolling5/6/uniform10 preserved better AUC/rank ordering in some deduped slices.
  - rolling4 lowered overconfidence but may lose discrimination on score-event transitions.
  - This reinforces keeping score-event and no-score drift model families separate.

Implementation decision
- Production startup Stage-1 cache is set to the conservative five completed prior seasons.
  - For 2026: 2021-2025.
- Rolling4 2022-2025 is the lead research candidate, not production.
- Uniform10 and weighted10 stay research-only.
