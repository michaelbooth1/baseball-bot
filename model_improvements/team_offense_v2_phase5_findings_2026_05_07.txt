Team Offense V2 -- Phase 5 Findings (Dollar Test)
====================================================

Date: 2026-05-07
Status: NO-GO under naive v1->v2 replacement.
        v2's structural correction is correct, but it interacts with
        the post-FV gate stack (gate_min_edge + gate_extreme_edge) in
        a way that the current gate thresholds were not calibrated
        for. Recommendation: SHADOW MODE + GATE RECALIBRATION before
        any promotion.


ARTIFACTS
---------
Runtime:        scripts/analysis/team_offense_model_v2.py
Shadow replay:  scripts/analysis/team_offense_v2_shadow_replay.py
Output:         data/analysis_output/team_offense_calibration/phase5_shadow_replay.json


METHOD
------
For all 119 placed bets in 2026 (live + paper combined) and 1,217 late-stage
skip rows, recompute Stage-3 logit-delta under v1 and v2 with the SAME
team-state context. Apply current gate config (edge_threshold=0.15,
edge_threshold_high_line=0.16, extreme_edge_max=0.22) to determine whether
each bet would have traded under each model.


TOP-LEVEL DISTRIBUTIONS
------------------------
v2-v1 logit-delta change across 119 bets:
  mean        -0.0047    (v2 slightly less aggressive overall)
  abs_mean     0.0671    (typical magnitude of swap)
  min         -0.2500    (strong negative shift in some matchups)
  max         +0.2501    (strong positive shift in others)

The absolute mean of 0.067 logit is substantial -- equivalent to roughly
1.5pp probability shift at typical placed-bet ask prices. v2 changes
edges materially per bet, in BOTH directions.

             ATH@NYY O8.5 inn4 (LOST, -$100)
    P&L change under v2: -$200.00

  NET P&L CHANGE UNDER v2 + CURRENT GATES: -$117.29


THE INTERACTION PROBLEM
------------------------
The 2 bets v2 newly trades reveal an important coupling:

  STL@WSH: v1 edge 0.160 -> v2 edge 0.200
           v1 BLOCKED by gate_min_edge (0.160 < 0.16 threshold for high lines)
           v2 PASSES both gates

  ATH@NYY: v1 edge 0.223 -> v2 edge 0.166
           v1 BLOCKED by gate_extreme_edge (0.223 > 0.22 cap from TR19)
           v2 PASSES both gates because v2 reduces the edge

Both bets lost $100 in production reality -- they were actually placed
under earlier gate config when extreme_edge_max=0.30 (TR16 era).

The structural issue: v2 changes the SHAPE and MAGNITUDE of the edge
distribution. The TR19 extreme_edge_max=0.22 cap was tuned EMPIRICALLY
against v1's edge distribution. When v2 makes some edges smaller (the
ATH@NYY case) and some edges larger (the STL@WSH case), the protective
calibration of the gate stack changes.

In particular:
  - gate_extreme_edge protects against high-edge "phantom run" cases
    where v1 produces edge > 0.22. v2 sometimes pulls these edges below
    the cap (e.g. via the negative b_prior). v2 LOSES the
    extreme_edge protection on those bets.
  - gate_min_edge requires edge >= 0.15/0.16 for trade. v2 sometimes
    raises borderline v1 edges above the threshold, ADDING bets v1
    would have skipped.

If v2 is the right Stage-3, the gates should be RECALIBRATED for it:
  - extreme_edge_max should likely be LOWER under v2 (~0.17-0.18) to
    match the protective behavior the 0.22 cap provided under v1
  - min_edge_threshold may need to be RAISED slightly under v2

WHAT'S CLEAN AND WHAT'S NOT
----------------------------
CLEAN:
  - v2's coefficients are structurally correct (Phases 1-4 + stability check)
  - Negative b_prior is robust across 8/8 sub-windows
  - On 1.13M-row residual table, v2 fit Brier-improves over v1

NOT CLEAN:
  - Naive v1->v2 swap with current gate config nets -$117 over 2026
  - Sample is small (4+2 = 6 decision-changing bets); even one outlier
    swings the result by $100
  - Gate stack was empirically tuned around v1; v2 changes the
    distribution gates see


WHY 2 BETS LOSING $100 EACH IS A BIG DEAL
------------------------------------------
We have 119 bets total in 2026 across live + paper. Net realized P&L for
those is roughly -$120 to -$150. Two -$100 v2-only-trades represent a
LARGE fraction of the year's P&L. Even a small probability that v2
correctly catches them (or a small change in the gate config to keep them
blocked) flips the dollar test.


RECOMMENDATIONS
---------------
1. Do NOT replace v1 with v2 in production yet.

2. Wire `--use-team-offense-v2-shadow` into signal_engine.py / live_engine.py
   so each placed-bet candidate row carries:
     - team_offense_delta_v1 (current production)
     - team_offense_delta_v2 (shadow)
   Live behavior remains v1; we collect comparison data continuously.

3. After ~30 days of shadow data:
   a. Recompute the gate-stack protective behavior under v2's edge
      distribution
   b. Find the v2-equivalent extreme_edge_max threshold (likely 0.17-0.18)
      that provides the same false-positive protection as v1's 0.22
   c. Find the v2-equivalent min-edge thresholds for std/high lines
   d. Re-run Phase 5 dollar test with the recalibrated thresholds

4. Phase 7 GO conditions become:
   - Brier improves on rolling validation window
   - Recalibrated gates produce non-negative dollar test
   - All 4 criteria pass

5. Optional: research a "soft" v2 deployment where the v1 delta and v2
   delta are blended (e.g. 50/50 average) as an interim risk-reducer
   while shadow data accumulates.


WHAT THIS DOESN'T CHANGE
-------------------------
The fundamental finding is unaffected: the current Stage-3 v1 is
structurally miscalibrated (Phase 1 + 4). Specifically:
  - LOGIT_DELTA_PER_RUN = 0.20 is too aggressive (~3.2x)
  - Hard clamps under-shrink mid-range teams
  - Linear inning weight ramp dials prior down too aggressively in
    mid-innings where most bets fire
  - Prior-season offense is anti-predictive once season+momentum is in

These are real and worth fixing. The Phase 5 result just says: fixing
them in isolation has unintended interactions with downstream gates.
Fix together, not separately.
