One important calibration note
My initial DEFAULT_LTP_ASK_GAP_MAX=0.30 (matching what I'd written in the session review) would have also blocked the WSH@NYM win (gap=0.47 > 0.30). The validation revealed this; I bumped the default to 0.50 so the gate only catches "screaming disconnect" cases (STL@PIT at 0.52) rather than "moderate disconnect" cases (WSH@NYM at 0.47). Documented the rationale inline so a future reader knows why the threshold isn't where the original review proposed it.

n=3 is way too small to declare these gates correctly tuned — the LATE_STAGE_SKIP_GATES membership means every future skip from these gates will produce a labeled feature row, so we'll have data to retune within a couple of weeks.
